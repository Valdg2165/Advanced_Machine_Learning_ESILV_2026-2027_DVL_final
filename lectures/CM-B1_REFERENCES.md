# CM-B1 — Formal References

Access date for all online resources: **2026-08-23**. Software versions match
the course runtime where specified.

   3rd ed., OTexts, 2021. https://otexts.com/fpp3/

2. Hyndman, R. J., & Athanasopoulos, G. “Time series cross-validation.” In
   *Forecasting: Principles and Practice*, 3rd ed., OTexts, 2021.

   https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2006.03.001

4. Ljung, G. M., & Box, G. E. P. “On a measure of lack of fit in time series

## Scope note

These references support the classical core: forecast contracts, temporal
validation, seasonal-naive baselines, ETS/SARIMA candidate interpretation,
residual diagnostics, prediction intervals, and error metrics. Deep forecasting
is reference-only in CM-B1.
