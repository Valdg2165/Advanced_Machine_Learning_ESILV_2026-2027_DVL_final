CM-B2 visual descriptions

These descriptions accompany the deterministic diagrams rendered in the student PDF. Their source is scripts/presentations/render_lecture_pdfs.py. The environment transcript is reproducible from shared/inventory_env.py with reset seed 11.

Slide 05 — Agent-environment loop

An agent sends an action to the environment. The environment returns the next state and reward. A separate evidence ledger records versions, seeds, costs, and limitations. The environment contract determines what a policy result can mean.

Slide 06 — MDP tuple

Five labelled boxes show state, action, transition, reward, and discount. Changing one component creates a different learning problem and invalidates comparisons against the previous environment.

Slide 08 — Environment contract and trace

The contract card states MultiDiscrete([9, 2]) observations, Discrete(3) actions, and reset state [1, 0] for seed 11. Four fixed actions 2, 1, 0, 2 yield next states [2, 0], [3, 0], [1, 0], and [3, 1], with rewards -1.50, -1.35, -0.30, and -1.80. Neither termination nor truncation occurs during these four steps.

Slide 09 — Reward cost ledger

Three boxes decompose reward into order, holding, and unmet-demand costs. A formula below them shows that reward is the negative sum of those costs. Omitted operational costs remain specification choices and incentives.

Slide 14 — Bellman action continuations

One state branches into three available actions. Each branch leads to an immediate reward plus discounted best next-state value. The first action is fixed before the later maximum is taken.

Slide 20 — Terminal-aware Q-learning target

A decision branch asks whether a transition truly terminated. If yes, the target is the immediate reward. Otherwise, including a time-limit truncation, the target adds the discounted next-state value. The executable definition is shared/rl.py:20-23.

Slide 21 — Auditable Q-learning update

Four connected boxes show the sequence old Q, target, temporal-difference error, and new Q. Each box states the operation to perform but withholds the numerical answer for the instructor-led debrief. The prompt separately asks what changes on genuine termination.

Slide 27 — Training/evaluation separation

A pipeline trains Q with exploration on development seeds, freezes the Q-table, and evaluates without exploration on disjoint seeds. Training return is not presented as final policy evidence.

Slide 28 — Fair policy comparison

Random actions, a transparent reorder heuristic, and a frozen learned policy enter one shared evaluator. The evaluator uses the same environment version, seeds, and KPIs, then reports distributions of cost, service, and uncertainty.

Slide 29 — Repeated policy outcomes

Three horizontal dot ranges show illustrative repeated outcomes for random, reorder, and frozen Q policies. Each row displays dispersion and a marked median. The fixed example teaches paired distribution comparison, not a claimed empirical ranking.
