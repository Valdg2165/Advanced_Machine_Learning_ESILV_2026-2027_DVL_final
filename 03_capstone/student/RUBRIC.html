Capstone Rubric — Operations & Decisions Portfolio

Stable-pair portfolio: 100 points
Module weight for this internal release: 40%, accepted by the course owner
Individual verification: separate 20% protocol; never inferred from pair marks

The mapping is exact:

  • scientific validity and comparisons: 37.5 portfolio = 15 module points;
  • reproducibility and claims-to-evidence: 37.5 portfolio = 15 module points;
  • operational recommendation, risks and limits: 25 portfolio points = 10 module points.

Scoring rules

  • Score release validity, evidence traceability and decision discipline, not fashionable methods or a leaderboard rank.
  • New fitting, policy training, tuning, sweeping or seed selection is outside scope. A faithful failed replay may earn full method credit when the failed state is preserved and the claim is bounded.
  • Instructor-issued forecast and RL receipts are institutional dependencies. Late return blocks the deadline and is not a student omission.
  • Support and Core routes share this rubric. Optional extensions are unnecessary for full marks and never compensate for invalid Core evidence.

Validity gates

  1. Scope: 3–6 claims, three required roles, and only evidence from the corresponding strand in the six-session sequence.
  2. Prior work: capstone training, tuning, model selection or policy repair earns no credit for the affected result.
  3. Replay: exactly two frozen commands; no third empirical replay.
  4. Controlled evidence: receipts bind the submitted forecast/Q-table; hidden targets/seeds are not present in the repository.
  5. Synthesis: separate component evidence is not end-to-end evidence.
  6. Integrity: fabrication/contribution concerns follow ESILV procedure, not an improvised mark penalty.

Name affected claim IDs and the earliest broken link. Do not apply an arbitrary whole-submission penalty.
