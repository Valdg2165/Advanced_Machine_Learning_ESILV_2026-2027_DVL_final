1. Purpose and hard boundary

This capstone curates the forecasting path built in TD1, TD2 and TDo1 and the reinforcement-learning path built in TD3, TD4 and TDo2. It asks whether that frozen six-session evidence can support a bounded operational decision. It does not ask for a new model, a joined forecast-to-policy system, or a seventh practical session.

Initialize the repository during TD1 and update its evidence index, provenance, contributions and AI-use record before leaving each of the six scheduled sessions. The capstone is a release review of those results, not a second grade for every implementation.

2. Signature evidence rule

Every performance claim must identify:

data or environment version + evaluation protocol + baseline + metric + repeated evidence + reproducible code and configuration + limitation

Every row in claims_evidence.csv must resolve to exact artifacts at the immutable submitted tag. “The model is better” and “the policy learned” are not claims; they omit the population, comparator and decision boundary.

3. Required claim set

Submit 3–6 claims. Exactly these three roles are mandatory; up to three supporting claims may add a narrow failure, interval or safety result. Every empirical claim must originate in the appropriate forecasting or RL session.

Required role Source Required evidence
forecasting_release TD1 + TD2 + TDo1 Trace the temporal/seasonal-naive foundation through rolling-origin model evidence to the frozen release, one failure period and the instructor-issued protected 28-day receipt.
rl_policy_release TD3 + TD4 + TDo2 Trace the MDP/reward and random/reorder baselines through frozen Q-learning to independent public evaluation, cost dispersion, fill rate and the instructor-issued controlled-batch receipt.
operational_safety_synthesis TS and RL release claims Conditional go/hold/no-go decision, named fallback, refusal boundary, and next evidence need.

The synthesis must cite the two source claim IDs. Forecast origins and RL episodes are different evaluation populations. The course has not shown that a TDo1 forecast feeds the TDo2 environment, so do not claim end-to-end controller performance, causal operational benefit, or production safety. A defensible release may recommend a limited trial, a hold, or no-go.

If prior evidence or a replay is invalid, keep the evidence and mark the claim partial, failed, or withdrawn. A negative result can be excellent work. Silent repair, post-hoc seed selection or replacement training cannot earn capstone credit.

Use the same repository across the existing six practical sessions:

6. Two-strand replay contract

Run exactly once for the release candidate from repository root:

python scripts/quick_replay.py --config configs/replay.yaml

The configuration contains exactly two frozen commands in order: forecasting, then reinforcement learning. They refresh one decisive public output per strand in at most ten minutes total. No command may fit, retrain, tune, sweep, change a protocol or select a seed. The runner hashes all declared scripts, configurations and data inputs, confines paths to the repository, rejects stale outputs and emits a success receipt only after both commands pass.

On genuine failure, preserve evidence/quick_replay_status.json, emit no success receipt, update affected claims, and set submission.quick_replay_receipt to not_applicable_after_failed_status. Do not repair the model during capstone.

7. Controlled evaluation

At TDo1, freeze artifacts/final_forecast.csv with exactly the public 28 dates and the course submission columns. The instructor scores it once against the protected horizon and returns a target-free receipt bound to the CSV hash.

At TDo2, freeze the non-pickle (9, 2, 3) artifacts/q_table.npy, configuration, evaluator and tag. The instructor evaluates it once on the common hidden batch and returns an aggregate, receipt and detached hashes. Hidden seeds and episode rows remain instructor-only.

The public replays exercise development contracts; they cannot recreate either protected evaluation. If an instructor receipt is late, the deadline remains blocked. Students are not penalized for an institutional handoff delay.

8. Release brief and declarations

The release brief is at most four equivalent pages, excluding references. It must include:

  1. decision context and release bounds;
  2. forecasting result, failure period and protected receipt;
  3. RL result, safety measure and protected receipt;
  4. conditional operational decision and transparent fallback;
  5. explicit non-integration statement, risks, refused claims and next evidence.

Use only CONTRIBUTIONS.csv for pair contributions and AI_USE_LOG.md for AI use. Log tool, purpose, retained material and human verification. Both students must be able to explain and reproduce every credited result.

9. Support, Core and Extension

All students are graded on the same Core evidence standard and can earn full marks without extensions.

  • Support: provided schemas, field examples, replay guardrails, checklist, office-hour diagnostics and guided checkpoints. Support does not reveal results or repair invalid evidence.
  • Core: the three required roles, two frozen replays, controlled receipts, traceability, synthesis and declarations.
  • Extension: optional sensitivity or ablation evidence already frozen in the relevant one of the six sessions. It cannot replace a missing Core result or raise the maximum grade.

10. Submission gate

Before immutable tagging, both students verify:

  • 3–6 claims and all three required roles;
  • every empirical claim traces only to the appropriate forecasting or RL sessions in the six-session sequence;
  • exactly two replay commands and a current success receipt, or an honest failed status with no success receipt;
  • protected receipts match the frozen artifact hashes;
  • every path exists and every placeholder is resolved;
  • the synthesis does not claim tested integration;
  • no hidden target, hidden seed, secret, personal data or instructor key is present;
  • AI and contribution declarations are complete.

Submit the immutable Git tag URL through the ESILV LMS. Formal late, absence, resit and integrity rules are those approved by ESILV, not invented here.
