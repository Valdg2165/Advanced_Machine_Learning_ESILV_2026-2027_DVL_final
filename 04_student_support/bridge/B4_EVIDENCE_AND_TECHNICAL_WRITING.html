B4 — Evidence and Technical Writing

Timebox: 30 minutes (5 min lesson/example, 20 min practice, 5 min exit check)
Status: ungraded support; do not submit this work

Why this matters

A defensible result sentence names the versioned data or environment, protocol, baseline, metric, repeated evidence, artifact path, and material limitation. It does not turn a development-origin difference into protected-horizon, industrial, or causal evidence.

Use this pattern:

Under [protocol and budget] on [versioned task/data], [candidate] differed from [baseline] by [metric result and repetitions]. This supports [bounded decision], but not [material limitation].

Worked micro-example

Suppose five development origins report mean MAE 8.4 for seasonal naive and 7.9 for ETS, with paired MAE differences ranging from -1.2 to 0.3 demand units. A defensible sentence is:

On daily_demand_synthetic_v1, across five frozen development origins with a 7-day horizon, ETS mean MAE was 7.9 versus 8.4 for seasonal naive; paired origin differences ranged from -1.2 to 0.3 units. This supports freezing ETS for the protected classroom release, but not superiority on every origin or transfer to real inventory operations.

The negative and positive paired differences expose non-uniform behaviour. The claim neither invents significance nor uses the protected horizon.

Practice — 20 minutes

Use this supplied map:

data/daily_demand_manifest.json
src/td2_forecast.py
configs/td2_rolling_origin.yaml
evidence/td2/per_origin.csv
evidence/td2/summary.json

The summary states:

{
  "dataset": "daily_demand_synthetic_v1",
  "split": "development_origins_only",
  "horizon_days": 7,
  "origins": 5,
  "metric": "MAE",
  "seasonal_naive_mean": 8.4,
  "ets_mean": 7.9,
  "paired_difference_range": [-1.2, 0.3]
}
  1. Write one claim of at most 65 words using the pattern above.
  2. Name the exact provenance, code, configuration, per-origin, and summary paths.
  3. Assign supported, partial, failed, or withdrawn; justify the status.
  4. State one decision the evidence supports and two claims it does not support.

Do not invent protected-horizon performance, a confidence interval, p-value, runtime, stock-cost benefit, or real-world effect.

Exit check — 5 minutes

  1. Can a reviewer locate evidence for every number?
  2. Does the limitation materially narrow the claim?
  3. What additional evidence is needed before an inventory-policy claim?

Mark B4 Independent when every path resolves in the supplied map and no statement exceeds the supplied evidence.
