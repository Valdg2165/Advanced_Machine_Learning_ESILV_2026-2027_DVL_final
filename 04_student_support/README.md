# Student Support — Advanced Machine Learning

This support pack helps students enter the same core course from different
starting points. It does not create an easier qualification for some students or
reserve high marks for those who arrived with more prior experience.

## Start here

1. If you have just received the ZIP, follow
   [Start here](../00_start/START_HERE.html)
   through environment preflight first.
2. Complete [`DIAGNOSTIC_AND_BRIDGE_PATHS.md`](DIAGNOSTIC_AND_BRIDGE_PATHS.html)
   before TD1.
3. Use the result to choose a temporary Foundation, Core Refresh, or Extension
   Preparation path.
4. Complete only the diagnostic-selected 30-minute units in
   [`bridge/README.md`](bridge/README.html); all five together are capped at 150
   minutes (2h30) and are ungraded.
5. Read [`SUPPORT_CORE_EXTENSION_MODEL.md`](SUPPORT_CORE_EXTENSION_MODEL.html)
   before the first applied session.
6. Before TD1, use
   [`TOPIC_REFRESH_TIME_SERIES.md`](TOPIC_REFRESH_TIME_SERIES.html) if temporal
   ordering, origins, or seasonal-naive forecasts are unfamiliar. Before TD3,
   use [`TOPIC_REFRESH_RL.md`](TOPIC_REFRESH_RL.html) if the MDP or Bellman update
   is unfamiliar. These refreshers are optional, ungraded, and do not replace
   the lecture or Core work.
7. Use [`templates/LEARNING_CHECKPOINT.md`](templates/LEARNING_CHECKPOINT.html)
   after each lab to identify the earliest
   unresolved concept or artifact.
8. When blocked, send a precise request using
   [`templates/HELP_REQUEST.md`](templates/HELP_REQUEST.html).
9. Use [`ACCESSIBILITY_AND_INCLUSIVE_USE.md`](ACCESSIBILITY_AND_INCLUSIVE_USE.html)
   for alternative course formats, accessible evidence, and the boundary
   between technical support and official accommodation.

## Non-negotiable principles

- The diagnostic is ungraded and does not appear on the transcript.
- Bridge practice and exit checks are not submitted or scored.
- A support route never caps the final grade.
- Students may change route at every topic.
- The assessed core outcomes are common to everyone.
- Advanced complexity cannot compensate for leakage, an unfair comparator, or
  an irreproducible experiment.
- A clean recovery checkpoint is legitimate support, not an answer key. Students
  must still explain and complete the assessed decisions.

## When to contact the teaching team

Contact the teaching team before TD1 if you cannot independently:

- launch the approved environment and execute a notebook;
- write and call a small Python function;
- inspect dataframe types and missing values;
- explain the difference between training and test data;
- save a result artifact to a declared path.

These gaps are repairable, but hiding them until a timed lab makes the course
less fair and less effective.

For disability, health, timing, attendance, or assessment-format needs, use the
official private ESILV accommodation channel. Do not put medical information in
the technical help template or pair repository.
