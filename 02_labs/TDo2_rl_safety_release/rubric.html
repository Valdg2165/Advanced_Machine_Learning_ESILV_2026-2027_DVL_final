TDo2 analytic rubric

This is a 20-point local rubric. Any later module rescaling remains subject to the approved ESILV assessment rule. A protocol defect removes credit only from the affected criterion until repaired and rerun; unaffected criteria remain assessable.

Points Criterion Full-credit evidence
4 Frozen artifact contract Q-table/config/code/environment identities and hashes match the submitted tag
4 Controlled protocol three unchanged policies share the same hidden episodes and final seeds remain undisclosed
5 Uncertainty and safety evidence cost/fill intervals plus unmet demand, stock, orders, and tail/failure evidence are interpreted
5 Release decision predeclared service/comparator rule yields an evidence-backed accept, conditional, or reject outcome
2 Auditability receipt, checksums, evidence ID, limitations, and contribution/AI records resolve

Cross-level grading rule

The common Core determines all 20 points. Support scaffolds do not reduce the attainable mark. Optional extensions add no points and are reviewed only after the Core is valid and reproducible. Prewritten assertions, shapes, and toy examples earn no credit without the student's completed decisions, results, and interpretation.

If the instructor-controlled aggregate/receipt is not returned during the session, the controlled-evidence criterion remains pending; instructor delay is not a student failure.
