Advanced Machine Learning practical sequence

The six assessed practical sessions cover only Time Series and Reinforcement Learning and form one progressive evidence chain.

Session Hours Focus Non-negotiable control
TD1 3 temporal data contract and baselines chronological target-safe boundary
TD2 3 rolling-origin ETS/SARIMA identical origins and horizon
TD3 3 inventory MDP, reward, baselines verified reward and common episodes
TD4 3 tabular Q-learning explicit loop and separate development evaluation
TDo1 3 forecast uncertainty and release protected 28-day targets remain hidden
TDo2 3 controlled RL safety release frozen Q-table and injected hidden seeds

Total: 12 hours TD + 6 hours TDo = 18 practical hours. The diagnostic is ungraded preparation and adds no contact hour. There is no seventh implicit session; the portfolio progressively curates evidence from these six.

TD1–TD4 contribute provisional development evidence and claim candidates. TDo1/TDo2 add the protected or controlled release evidence that confirms, revises, or rejects those candidates; all six sessions therefore contribute to the capstone without creating an extra practical session.
