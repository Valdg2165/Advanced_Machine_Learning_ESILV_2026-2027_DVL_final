TDo1 analytic rubric

This is a 20-point local rubric. Any later module rescaling remains subject to the approved ESILV assessment rule. A protocol defect removes credit only from the affected criterion until repaired and rerun; unaffected criteria remain assessable.

Points Criterion Full-credit evidence
4 Evidence continuity TD2 origins/orders/point metrics are restored without post-hoc tuning
5 Uncertainty evaluation 80%/95% coverage and width use common cases and correct bounds
4 Diagnostics and limits residual, convergence, censoring, and finite-sample limits materially bound the claim
5 Release artifact 28 exact dates, valid point/bounds schema, no target leakage, model ID, and hash
2 Operational decision selection/fallback rule and planning implication are explicit and reproducible

Cross-level grading rule

The common Core determines all 20 points. Support scaffolds do not reduce the attainable mark. Optional extensions add no points and are reviewed only after the Core is valid and reproducible. Prewritten assertions, shapes, and toy examples earn no credit without the student's completed decisions, results, and interpretation.

If the instructor-controlled final-horizon receipt is unavailable, grade the valid release artifact and handoff; instructor delay is not a student failure.
